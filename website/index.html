What it does

A Pokédex that answers the next question.

Not just stats on a page. Dexplorer shows how Pokémon connect, what beats what, and whether your team holds up.

  • Find any Pokémon

    Search by name or number, filter by any of the 18 types, and sort by number, name or total power. Every filter covers all 1,025 Pokémon.

  • Follow every evolution

    Evolution lines are drawn as trees, so Eevee's eight branches all fit. Each arrow says how it happens: “Level 16”, “Thunder Stone”, “Friendship, night”.

  • Know the matchups

    See what each Pokémon is weak to, what it resists and what can't touch it, worked out for both of its types.

  • Build a balanced team

    Pick six. A weak-spot table checks your team against all 18 attack types and flags the ones nobody covers.

  • Works without signal

    Every Pokémon you open is saved on your phone, artwork included. On a train, on a plane, out of range: it still opens.

  • Comfortable to use

    Light and dark mode, shiny colors, favorites, gentle haptics, and support for TalkBack, larger text and reduced motion.

Evolution trees

See the whole family at once.

Some Pokémon evolve in one straight line. Others branch. Dexplorer draws both, and tapping any Pokémon in the tree takes you straight to it.

  • The About section changes as you move along the line
  • Baby Pokémon and hidden abilities are marked
  • Every evolution method in plain words

Team builder

Find the gap before your opponent does.

Add up to six Pokémon. Dexplorer counts how many of them are weak to, or resist, each attack type, and tells you in plain words where you need cover.

  • “Needs cover” flags, not just colors
  • Average stats for the whole team
  • Add from the list or from any Pokémon's page
